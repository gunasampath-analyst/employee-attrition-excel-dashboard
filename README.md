# 👥 Employee Attrition Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

An **Excel dashboard** created to analyze employee attrition and understand workforce trends.

The project focuses on employee demographics, job satisfaction, overtime, salary, job roles, and employee experience.

## 🎯 Objective

* Analyze employee attrition
* Identify departments and job roles with high attrition
* Understand the impact of overtime and job satisfaction
* Analyze employee salary and experience
* Identify workforce trends

## 🛠️ Tool

**Microsoft Excel**

### Features Used

* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts
* Slicers
* Excel Formulas
* KPI Cards
* Data Visualization

## 📊 KPIs

* Total Employees
* Employees Left
* Attrition Rate
* Average Monthly Income
* Average Years at Company

## 📈 Analysis

* Attrition by Department
* Attrition by Job Role
* Attrition by Age Group
* Overtime vs Attrition
* Income vs Attrition
* Years at Company vs Attrition
